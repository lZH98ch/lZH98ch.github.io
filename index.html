Curriculum Vitae
I am a postdoctoral fellow at IAS supervised by Prof. Bhargav Bhatt. My research interests lie in arithmetic geometry,
particularly in p-adic cohomology theories, p-adic Hodge theory and their applications to arithmetic problems.
I obtained my Ph.D. from Sorbonne Université, directed by Prof. Wieslawa Niziol. Previously, I was a member of the ALGANT program.
Before that, I obtained my bachelor's degree from Nanjing University.
